HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Manage your team's Morale, Productivity, and Performance over 10 rounds. The player with the highest combined total wins.

EACH TURN

Draw a scenario card, then pick how to respond:

  • SAFE — Easy question (30s timer). Correct: +1 to one stat. Wrong or timeout: no penalty.
  • KNOWLEDGE — Answer a question (20s timer). Correct: +2/+1 to stats. Wrong: −1.
  • BOLD — Harder question (15s timer). Correct: +3. Wrong: −2. High stakes.

AUDITS

Once per game window, skip your turn to challenge another player with a Knowledge question. If they answer correctly, you lose 1 Performance. If they answer wrong, you gain 1 Performance. Audits reset at round 6 — use them or lose them.

EVENTS

At rounds 4, 7, and 10, an Event Card fires and affects all players. Events can shift the standings dramatically — no one is safe.

WINNING

Highest total stats after round 10 wins. Ties broken by Performance first, then Morale. If all tiebreakers are equal, it's a shared victory.
